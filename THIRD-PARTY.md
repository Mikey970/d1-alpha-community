The release includes Xenia, the web-tiger backend, Node.js, Python/Tk, PowerShell,
Microsoft's Visual C++ runtime and 7-Zip. Their notices are in `licenses/`,
the installed dependency folders and the bundled runtime folders.

Xenia: https://github.com/craftycodie/xenia-canary

Backend: https://github.com/Blam-Network/web-tiger

7-Zip 25.01: https://github.com/ip7z/7zip/releases/tag/25.01
The corresponding unmodified source archive is included in `source/7zip`.

No license for this repository overrides third-party terms. In particular,
